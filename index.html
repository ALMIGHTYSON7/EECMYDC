About Our Church

EECMYDC

Ethiopian Evangelical Church Mekane Yesus – Dilla Congregation is a Christian community committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ and serving people through faith, fellowship, and love. The congregation provides a place where believers come together for worship, prayer, Bible study, and spiritual growth. It also seeks to support families, young people, children, and the wider community through Christian teaching and practical service.

Our mission is to help people know Jesus Christ, grow in faith, live according to God's Word, and become a blessing to their community. Through worship, discipleship, evangelism, and community service, the Dilla Congregation continues to share God's love and hope with everyone.
